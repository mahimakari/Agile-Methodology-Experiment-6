Agile methodology is a flexible project management and product development approach that breaks work into small, repeating cycles called iterations.
Instead of following a rigid, long-term plan, Agile focuses on continuous collaboration, fast feedback, and delivering small usable parts of a project frequently. 
It originated in software development but is now used across many industries like marketing, finance, and design.
